How the game works

The board is a grid of cells. Each cell is alive (filled) or dead (empty). Once per generation, every cell updates at the same moment, based on the eight cells that touch it, diagonals included.

Good to know

  • Wrap around edges joins the left edge to the right and the top to the bottom, so a glider that leaves one side comes back on the other.
  • The Rule menu writes rules in B/S notation. The digits after B are the neighbor counts that bring a dead cell to life, and the digits after S are the counts that keep a live cell alive. Choose a preset or type your own, and this explanation changes to match.
  • Shade cells by age changes a cell's color the longer it stays alive, so you can see which parts of a pattern are new.
