How the game works

The board is a grid of live (filled) and dead (empty) cells. Each generation, every cell updates at once, based on the eight cells that touch it, diagonals included.

  • Wrap around edges joins left to right and top to bottom, so a glider that leaves one side comes back on the other.
  • In the Rule menu, B lists the neighbor counts that bring a dead cell to life and S lists the counts that keep a live cell alive.
